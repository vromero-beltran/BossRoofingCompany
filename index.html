Common roofing questions

What types of roofing services do you provide?

Roof repairs, full roof replacements, and roof evaluations — plus siding, skylights, decks, and gutters. One crew for your whole exterior.

How can I tell if my roof needs repair or a full replacement?

Small, isolated damage — a few bad shingles, one leak — usually just needs a repair. If the roof is old, leaking in multiple spots, or you've already patched it a few times, replacement is usually the smarter money. We'll give you an honest read either way.

What are the warning signs that indicate a roof replacement is necessary?

Curling or missing shingles, granules piling up in the gutters, water stains on your ceilings, sagging spots, or a roof pushing 20+ years. Any of those means it's time for a real inspection.

How do I get a roofing estimate and what does it include?

Fill out the estimate form on this page or call/text (925) 290-8447. We'll come look at the roof and give you a straight price covering materials, labor, and timeline — no surprises.

How quickly can an inspection or estimate be scheduled?

Usually within a few days. If it's leaking right now, tell us and we'll move you up.

What happens during a roof inspection?

We walk the whole roof — shingles, flashing, vents, gutters — and check the attic for leaks and ventilation issues. Then you get a plain-English rundown of what's wrong and what it'll take to fix it.

How long does a typical roof replacement take?

Most homes take two to five days depending on size and material. We show up, tear off, install, and clean up — you get a clear timeline before we start.

Do you offer energy-efficient or specialty roofing systems?

Yes — reflective "cool" roofing and metal roofing that cut heat and last longer. Ask us what's actually worth it for your house.

Do you service my local area?

We're based in San Francisco and work across the Bay Area — Concord, Walnut Creek, Fremont, Hayward, and surrounding cities. Not sure? Call and ask.

What types of roofing materials do you install?

Asphalt shingles, tile, metal, and flat roofing systems. We'll walk you through the trade-offs and help you pick what fits your house and budget.

How much should I expect to pay for a roofing project?

Depends on size, material, and how complex the roof is. That's exactly why the estimate is free — after we see it, you'll know the full cost before any work starts.
